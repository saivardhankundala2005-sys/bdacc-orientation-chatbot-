[SECTION 1: ABOUT & FOUNDING HISTORY]
The Big Data Analytics & Consulting Cell (BDACC) is the premier student-led technical and consulting club at National Institute of Technology Warangal (NITW).
- Founded Year: 2019
- Founding Faculty Advisor: Dr. R. B. V. Subramaanyam (Head of Computer Science & Engineering)
- First General Secretary: K. Sai Vamsi (Batch of 2021, CSE)
- Motto: "Transforming raw data into strategic decisions."

[SECTION 2: CORE DOMAINS & TEAMS]
BDACC operates across four specialized student wings:
1. Data Engineering & Analytics: Big Data pipelines, PySpark, SQL databases, distributed computing.
2. Artificial Intelligence & ML: Deep learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, LLM agent development.
3. Management & Strategy Consulting: Case studies, market entry strategy, product management frameworks (RICE, MoSCoW, MECE).
4. Product & UI/UX Design: Wireframing, product teardowns, user experience architecture, design systems.

[SECTION 3: SIGNATURE EVENTS & INITIATIVES]
- Datathon (Annual Flagship): NIT Warangal's largest 36-hour data science hackathon with real-world industry datasets.
- Case Craft: National-level case study competition testing consulting frameworks and financial modeling.
- AI Lab Orientation: Interactive hands-on session introducing first-year students to building LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and Prompt Engineering.
- Weekly Paper Reading Groups: Discussion circle reviewing latest arXiv preprints in Machine Learning and Systems architecture.

[SECTION 4: RECRUITMENT & MEMBERSHIP]
- BDACC inducts freshers through a two-phase process: Aptitude & Domain Test followed by a Case/Coding Teardown interview.
- Active membership consists of over 120 students across B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programs at NIT Warangal.
